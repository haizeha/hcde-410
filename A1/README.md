# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/Burke-Gilman-Trail-counter-north-of-NE-70th-St/2z5v-ecg8  (pulled 2026-10-12)
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain, as listed on the dataset's page on data.seattle.gov. Free to use, modify, and share; attribution to the City of Seattle is given here as good practice.

## Questions I asked
- When is the trail busiest, and when is it quietest?
- Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day?
- Is there evidence people commute on the trail, or is it mostly recreation? (weekday vs. weekend)
- Do mornings and evenings flow in opposite directions?

## How to re-run
Open `A1.ipynb` and run all cells (needs `requests`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`).
